Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: everything you do in Ordbok stays on your phone. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and the app has no ads or analytics.

Who we are

Ordbok is a Swedish–English dictionary and learning app for Android, made by an independent developer. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Ordbok. You can contact us at pratikthapa485@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

Ordbok saves the following on your device so the app works and remembers your progress:

  • Your account: the name and email address you enter when you sign up, and your password in a protected (salted and hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Your learning: saved words, recently viewed words, your practice deck, level test result, XP, levels and practice history.
  • Your profile: the name and profile photo you choose.
  • Your settings: theme, notification time and similar preferences.

This information is stored only on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Because we never receive it, we cannot see, recover or move it to another phone.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or usage statistics. Ordbok contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Photos

If you choose a profile photo, the app only reads the picture you select. It is resized and stored on your phone and is not uploaded.

Pronunciation

Words are read aloud by your phone’s own text-to-speech engine. Ordbok does not send the words you look up anywhere. The speech engine is provided by your phone’s maker or Google and is covered by their privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on the word of the day, the reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Ordbok Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google Play only tells the app whether your subscription is active. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment.

Sharing and feedback

“Share Ordbok” and “Send feedback” use your phone’s own share sheet and email app. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not share them.

Children

Ordbok is made for learners of all ages, including children. It works the same way for everyone: the app does not collect, transmit or share personal information from anyone, children included. The name, email address and photo a child may enter stay on the phone and never reach us or any third party.

Ordbok has no advertising, no analytics and no chat or social features. Parents and guardians can delete everything the app has stored by clearing its data in the phone’s settings or uninstalling it, and can contact us at the address below with any questions.

Your rights

You are in full control of your data: you can change it in the app, and delete all of it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling Ordbok. If you have questions about your privacy or want to exercise your rights under the GDPR, email us at the address above.

Deleting your account and data

Your Ordbok account and everything linked to it exist only on your phone. To delete them:

  • open your phone’s Settings → Apps → Ordbok → Storage and tap Clear data, or
  • uninstall Ordbok.

This permanently deletes your account (name, email and password), saved words, progress, profile and settings. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete on our side. If you have questions, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If Ordbok starts handling data differently, for example by adding online accounts, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
